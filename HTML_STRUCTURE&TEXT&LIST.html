Scrambble Eggs

Eggs are one of my favorite foods. Here is a recipe for delicously rich scrambled eggs.

Ingridients

  • 2 eggs
  • 1tbs butter
  • 2tbs cream

Method

  1. Melt butter in a fring pan over medium heat
  2. Gently mix the eggs and cream in a bowl
  3. Once butter has melted add creem and eggs
  4. Using a spatula fold the eggs from the edge o the pan to the center every 20 seconds (as if you are making an omelette )
  5. Whwn the eggs are still moist remove from the heat ( it will continue to cook on the plate until served)
